Our Story

Hey there! .. I’m Thamahrai Mark Sweets, born in the heart of Madurai back in 1968. I started as a tiny sweet stall under a simple rooftop that often leaked during rainy nights ☔, yet those days were filled with warmth, laughter, and the pure aroma of melting jaggery 🍬. Every corner of mine holds memories of those days when the sweetness was crafted by hand and packed with heart ❤. My pride still lies in the same old traditional mud stove 🔥 that has been my heart since day one. Every week, it’s carefully layered with wet mud to keep it alive never replaced, only cherished. That’s where my authentic taste is born the secret that no modern machine can ever recreate 😋.

And here’s my little brag , no sugar, ever! ❌ Just pure, golden jaggery, roasted peanuts, sesame, or coconut , mixed, molded, and made with love 💛. Take a bite, and you’ll feel that rush of flavor, the kind that makes your mouth water and your heart melt instantly ✨. Over time, I’ve dressed up stronger walls, brighter space, and modern packing 📦 but my soul remains unchanged. I’m still the same Thamahrai Mark Sweets that carries the legacy of Madurai’s tradition and the taste of your childhood memories. Because I’m not just a sweet shop… I’m a story of generations, crafted in jaggery and love. 💫

“Tradition never gets old rather it just tastes sweeter with time.” 🍯❤
