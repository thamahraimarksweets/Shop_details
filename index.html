Our Story

Thamahrai Mark Sweets is a family-run shop that brings traditional Tamil village recipes to the town. We use only good jaggery and hand-roasted peanuts — no preservatives. Our recipes are passed down, and every batch is tested for crispiness.

  • Fresh daily batches
  • Local sourcing of peanuts & coconut
  • Festival specials & custom gift boxes
